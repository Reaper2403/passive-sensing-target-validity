The Android summaries are privacy-safe extracts of local audit outputs. They support
checking the displayed arithmetic and regenerating Appendix C figures, **not** an
independent rerun from raw phone logs. The September saved-model freeze was not
independently established, and its MAE interval includes zero.

Report figures are regenerated by `report/make_figures.py` from these aggregate
files. The participant-flow diagram uses audited counts explicitly recorded in that
script. The report PDF remains the source of record for definitions, exclusions,
model specifications, and limitations.
